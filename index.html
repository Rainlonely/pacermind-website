BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

A few things
to know.

Where can I use PacerMind?

PacerMind is available for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Check the App Store listing for current device and OS requirements.

Can I bring my existing runs?

Yes. Authorize Apple Health, or connect Strava, to bring supported workouts into PacerMind.

Is AI required to keep a running journal?

Your records, notes and routes are local-first. AI features are optional and ask for your consent before sharing the context needed for a request.

Where can I find current pricing?

The App Store and the app show current pricing and any available in-app purchases. You can also contact the maker if you have questions.
